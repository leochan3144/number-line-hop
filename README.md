# Number Line Hop

A two-level number line game for P.4–P.6 students.

- **Level 1 · Pip's Arrow Hop**: match each directed number with its vector. Drag the arrow that starts at 0 and ends on the target, and Pip the bunny hops along it one unit at a time.
- **Level 2 · Freddie's Double Jump**: add and subtract directed numbers by joining two vectors head to tail. The **Highlight terms** button shades Term 1 and Term 2 (with their signs) in matching colours.

Play it: https://leochan3144.github.io/number-line-hop/

Everything is in `index.html`. The questions are the `L1Q` and `L2Q` lists near the top of the script.
